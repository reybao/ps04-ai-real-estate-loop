# Source Notes — Piece 03: AI infrastructure’s bottlenecks are not just about power

Data Center Knowledge reports that Finnish regulators told Google to halt major site work at two planned data centers after substantial site preparation occurred before required environmental impact assessments were completed; the intervention did not cancel the projects.

## 03-c4

The evidence suggests that project execution can face constraints beyond grid access: the Finland report describes a work halt despite reported grid access, while Utility Dive describes labor, equipment, and regulatory challenges. A keynote speaker separately cautions that land near transmission lines, utility applications, and announced campuses do not by themselves establish delivered capacity or an operating data center. This comparison does not establish that the same rules or causes apply across jurisdictions.
