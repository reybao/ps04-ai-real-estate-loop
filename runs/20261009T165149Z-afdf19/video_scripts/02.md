Now to a different situation in Texas. ThisWay Global says it acquired the unfinished RELLIS project and plans to convert it into a phased Research and AI Compute Center totaling 73 megawatts, on about 25 acres of Texas A&M System land.

The company says deployment is tied to confirmed power availability and applicable utility approvals. That wording is important. It describes dependencies, not proof that the planned capacity will be delivered. The reporting we reviewed does not disclose the requested utility capacity or the approval status. For diligence, that means separating land control from power delivery—and separating a stated project plan from completed approvals and operating equipment. A number in a project announcement is not the same thing as a commissioned campus.
